The Business Case

Why Every Company Needs a Cancer Wellness Program

Cancer is now the second leading cause of workplace absenteeism in India. Yet most organisations have no proactive plan. The cost of late diagnosis — in lost productivity, insurance claims, and human suffering — is immense and preventable.

Early Detection = 5x Better Survival

Stage 1 cancers have >90% survival rates when caught early through regular screening. Without screening, most are found at Stage 3 or 4.

Reduce Insurance & Healthcare Costs

Preventive screening programs reduce catastrophic medical claims by up to 40%, directly lowering group insurance premiums for your company.

Boost Retention & Employer Brand

Employees who feel cared for stay longer. Wellness benefits rank in the top 3 factors employees consider when choosing — or staying at — a company.
